B.11 DONOR IMPACT - PROPOSED QUERY PREVIEW
==============================================================================
The existing view counts delivery allocations regardless of
delivery status. Below: the same donors under the proposed query
(DELIVERED only, CANCELLED excluded from all three aggregates,
spoiled kept separate) and the existing view. Nothing is created
or modified; this is a SELECT preview.

donor                        donated DELIVERED  spoiled  undistrib.  view says
Demo Donor                     25.00     17.50     0.00        7.50      17.50
Restaurant Donor               17.00     17.00     0.00        0.00      17.00
Hotel Donor                    20.00     15.00     1.50        3.50      15.00
Bakery Donor                   14.00     14.00     0.00        0.00      14.00

  0 of 4 donor(s) differ between the proposed
  query and the existing view (the gap is un-DELIVERED allocations).

  Definitions used here:
    CANCELLED donations are excluded from ALL THREE aggregates, so a
    cancelled donation contributes nothing to donated, delivered or
    spoiled - the denominators cannot disagree with each other.
    total_donated_kg = SUM(donation_item.quantity_kg) over non-cancelled
                       donations
    delivered_kg     = SUM(delivery_item.quantity_delivered_kg) where
                       delivery.delivery_status = 'DELIVERED' and the
                       donation is not CANCELLED
    spoiled_kg       = SUM(spoilage_record.quantity_spoiled_kg) over
                       non-cancelled donations, kept separate so it is
                       never counted as impact
    undistributed_balance_kg = donated - delivered - spoiled. This is an
                       ACCOUNTING BALANCE, not a physical stock figure:
                       the food may be in transit, awaiting inspection,
                       or already gone without a recorded delivery or
                       spoilage entry. Label it 'undistributed balance',
                       never 'on hand' or 'remaining stock'.
